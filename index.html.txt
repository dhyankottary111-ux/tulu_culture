<!-- ABOUT -->
<section id="about" class="section">
  <h2>About <span class="blue">Tulunadu</span></h2>
  <p>
    Tulunadu is a culturally rich coastal region of South India, known for
    its ancient traditions, rituals, language, and strong community values.
    The Tuluva people have preserved their heritage through generations.
  </p>
</section>

<!-- TRADITIONS -->
<section id="traditions" class="section soft">
  <h2>Traditional <span class="blue">Arts & Rituals</span></h2>

  <div class="grid">
    <div class="card">
      <h3><span class="blue">Bhuta Kola</span></h3>
      <p>
        A sacred ritual where divine spirits (Daivas) are worshipped.
        Performers embody the spirit using elaborate costumes and rituals.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3><span class="blue">Pili Vesha</span></h3>
      <p>
        A vibrant tiger dance performed during festivals. Dancers paint
        themselves as tigers and perform to rhythmic drum beats.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3><span class="blue">Yakshagana</span></h3>
      <p>
        A traditional dance-drama combining music, dialogue, and colorful
        costumes to narrate stories from epics.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="card">
      <h3><span class="blue">Kambala</span></h3>
      <p>
        A traditional buffalo race conducted in muddy fields, symbolizing
        agricultural heritage and community spirit.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<!-- FOOD -->
<section id="food" class="section">
  <h2>Traditional <span class="blue">Cuisine</span></h2>
  <p>
    Tulu cuisine reflects simplicity, coastal ingredients, and bold flavors.
    Rice, coconut, and spices form the heart of everyday meals.
  </p>

  <ul class="food-list">
    <li><span class="blue">Neer Dosa</span> – soft rice crepes</li>
    <li><span class="blue">Kori Rotti</span> – chicken curry with crispy rotti</li>
    <li><span class="blue">Bangude Pulimunchi</span> – spicy mackerel curry</li>
  </ul>
</section>
